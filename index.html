How to Choose a Durable Everyday Ring: Tips From a Jewelry Factory

We manufacture fine jewelry in Panyu, Guangzhou. One question customers ask us every day: which gold purity is right for a ring you wear every single day?

14K vs 18K at a glance

14K18K
Gold content58.3%75%
DurabilityHarder, better for daily wearSofter, scratches and dents more easily
PriceLowerHigher
Best forRings worn 24/7Occasional pieces

Our honest recommendation: for an engagement or wedding ring that never comes off, choose 14K. It is harder, holds its prongs, and survives knocks far better. Save 18K for pieces that see lighter use.
